# WorldRole free edition: service terms and limits

Updated 2026-10-24. Maintainer: [zzn199216](https://github.com/zzn199216).

WorldRole's marketplace package and the optional public-source companion are free to install and use. This edition includes no payment processing or required paid WorldRole subscription. Your AI client, model provider, network provider, and third-party tools may charge separately.

The hosted free service currently allows guests 20 distinct returned jobs per network per UTC day and free accounts 50 per account per UTC day. Request-rate, concurrency, response-size, and byte limits also apply. The server's usage response is authoritative for an account's current allowance and any existing grants. Free use does not mean unlimited access or guaranteed availability. Free registration and sharing are optional; do not bypass limits or misrepresent identities. The local public-source companion does not spend WorldRole hosted-job quota but must respect each source's access rules and limits.

Job results may be incomplete, cached, expired, or incorrect. Verify the original employer listing, eligibility, compensation, visa conditions, and application destination. A remote label does not imply worldwide eligibility. Matching scores are prioritization aids, not hiring probabilities or guarantees. WorldRole does not guarantee interviews, employment, visa approval, or employer conduct.

The hosted MCP does not send emails or submit applications. Optional agent/client tools operate only under the user's authorization and their own capabilities. Do not send unlawful content, credentials, or unrelated personal data through job searches. Third-party job data remains subject to its owners' terms; the software license does not transfer rights to that data.
